## 🇨🇳 中文

以首席营销策划官的视角，把用户模糊的业务诉求转化为资深操盘手会交付的实战级营销方案。覆盖品牌认知、产品上市、内容种草、活动战役、增长转化、客户经营、渠道动销、整合营销等全案类型。默认以飞书文档交付，文档内包含正文、可视化图表与图片，用户拿到后无需二次返工。方案可直接用于四种下一步：内部研讨、对上汇报、对外提案、对内拆解执行。当需求不明或不属于营销策划时，它不创建文档，而是用一两句话追问并说明自身职能。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 一句模糊业务诉求 → 先锁定营销场景再产出全案
- 覆盖品牌认知/产品上市/内容种草/活动战役/增长转化/客户经营/渠道动销/整合营销
- 默认输出飞书文档 → 正文 + 可视化图表 + 图片一体交付
- 按四种用途组织内容 → 内部研讨、对上汇报、对外提案、对内拆解执行
- 需求不明时先追问再动手 → 不生成空壳文档
- 支持在已有方案上改写与优化 → 沿用同一套交付规格

## 🇬🇧 English

A chief-marketing-officer skill that turns a vague business request into a campaign plan a seasoned operator would actually ship. It covers brand awareness, product launch, content seeding, campaign battles, growth and conversion, customer operations, channel sell-through and integrated marketing. Output lands as a Feishu doc containing body copy, visual charts and images, so no second pass is needed. The deliverable is ready for four next steps: internal discussion, reporting upward, pitching externally, and breaking down execution internally. When the request is unclear or out of scope, it creates no doc and instead asks one or two targeted questions.

### ✨ What it does

- One vague business ask → scenario locked first, then a full plan produced
- Covers brand awareness, product launch, content seeding, campaigns, growth, customer operations and channel sell-through
- Ships as a Feishu doc → body, charts and images in one deliverable
- Structured for four uses → internal review, upward reporting, client pitch, execution breakdown
- Asks before building when the request is unclear → no hollow documents
- Rewrites and optimizes existing plans → same delivery spec throughout
